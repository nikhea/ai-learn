# Mission: Modern LLM & AI Engineering

## Why
Mike (thespider) is a developer in Los Angeles looking to build AI-powered applications, potentially pivot into an AI engineering career, and explore this technology out of curiosity. He wants practical, applicable knowledge he can use to ship real projects and potentially change careers.

## Success looks like
- Build and deploy at least one LLM-powered application (e.g., RAG system, AI agent, or AI-integrated workflow)
- Understand prompt engineering well enough to reliably get desired outputs from LLMs
- Know when to use prompting, RAG, or fine-tuning for different scenarios
- Have a clear path toward an AI engineering career with identified skill gaps

## Constraints
- Learning in spare time while working as a developer
- Prefers practical, hands-on learning over pure theory
- Based in Los Angeles (consider this for community suggestions)

## Out of scope
- Deep research into LLM architecture/training (focus on application engineering)
- Machine learning theory/math (focus on practical usage)
- Building models from scratch (use existing APIs and frameworks)
